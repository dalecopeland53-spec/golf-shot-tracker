HOW TO

Set your numbers, trigger the tracer and compare the result.
1 · Ball Speed

Set the shot speed. More speed usually means more distance.

2 · Launch Angle

Lower values fly flatter. Higher values launch higher.

3 · Spin Rate

Set RPM to change how the ball stays in the air.

4 · Spin Axis

Negative and positive values curve in opposite directions. Zero is straight.

5 · Tracer Trigger

Tap it to calculate and draw the shot. Carry, Total, Apex and Offline update above.

KISS: Change one setting at a time. Trigger the tracer, compare the result, then make the next change.

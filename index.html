HOW TO

Simple guide to the DRC Elite Golf Shot Tracker. Set your numbers, trigger the tracer and read the result.

1Set Ball Speed

Move the Ball Speed slider to match the speed of the shot. Higher speed normally produces more distance.

2Set Launch Angle

Choose the launch angle of the ball. Lower angles fly flatter; higher angles launch the ball higher.

3Set Spin Rate

Enter the spin rate in RPM. Spin changes how the ball stays in the air and how the trajectory develops.

4Set Spin Axis

Use Spin Axis to represent curve. Negative and positive values bend the tracer in opposite directions. Zero is a straight-axis shot.

5Tap Tracer Trigger

The app calculates the trajectory and draws the shot path on screen. Adjust any slider and trigger again to compare shots.

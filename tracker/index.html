How To Use The App

Ball Flight
Tap the shot shape you saw. Read the likely cause and simple fix.
Practice
Choose a club, then tap Good, OK or Bad after each shot. The graph updates automatically.
Round
Enter each hole score, then tap Save Round. Your total, holes played and average update automatically.
My Clubs
Edit the club list to match your bag and tap Save Clubs. Practice totals are shown by club.
My Notes
Type the swing thoughts or feels that work and tap Save Notes.
Your Data
Practice, round, club and notes data are saved on this device.
